Welcome to Stone Ocean

Stone Ocean is the sixth part of JoJo's Bizarre Adventure. It follows Jolyne Cujoh, who is sent to Green Dolphin Street Prison after being framed for a crime.

Meet the Characters

Jolyne Cujoh is the main character of Stone Ocean. She discovers her Stand ability, Stone Free, and uses it to survive prison.

Ermes Costello becomes one of Jolyne's closest friends. Her Stand, Kiss, gives her the ability to create stickers that duplicate objects.

Foo Fighters is a unique being made from plankton who becomes one of Jolyne's allies.

The Story

Jolyne must uncover the truth behind her imprisonment while facing dangerous enemies and protecting the people she cares about. Her journey takes her through battles, friendship, and unexpected discoveries.
