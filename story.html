Meet the Characters

Jolyne Cujoh is the daughter of Jotaro Kujo. After being framed for a crime, she is sent to Green Dolphin Street Prison.

Ermes Costello becomes one of Jolyne's closest friends. She uses her Stand, Kiss, to help fight their enemies.

Foo Fighters, also known as F.F., is a unique being who becomes one of Jolyne's allies. F.F. uses a Stand-powered ability to protect her friends.

The Story

Stone Ocean follows Jolyne as she discovers her Stand, Stone Free, and fights to survive inside Green Dolphin Street Prison.

With the help of her friends, Jolyne uncovers a dangerous plan connected to her father and the mysterious Enrico Pucci.
